Privacy Policy

Chenny LoL Tracker · Last updated 26 October 2026

Chenny LoL Tracker is a Discord Activity and web page that shows the public League of Legends match history of one player (FullClearDeluxe#FarmR) with commentary. This policy explains what data it handles.

What we collect from you

Nothing. The Activity does not ask Discord for your identity, does not log you in, and does not store any information about the people who open it. Discord itself handles launching the Activity under Discord's own privacy policy.

Data shown in the Activity

Match results, ranks, champion picks and summoner names shown in the Activity come from publicly available League of Legends statistics (as shown on op.gg). Summoner names of other players appear only because they took part in the tracked player's public games.

Cookies and storage

The Activity does not use cookies or tracking. Your browser may remember your music volume setting locally on your own device; it never leaves your device.

Third parties

Champion and item images are loaded from op.gg's public image servers. The web version may play background music through YouTube, which is subject to YouTube's own privacy policy.

Children

The Activity is intended for Discord users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated from time to time; the date above shows the latest version. Questions or removal requests can be raised in the Chenny LoL Tracker Discord server.
